A cozy packing puzzle

Little boxes.
Thoughtful gifts.

A place for every candy. A little thought in every parcel.

Drag and rotate candy shapes to fill a gift box. Match the stamped windows, follow the order, and discover the arrangement that makes everything fit.

50 puzzles

Five ribbon-wrapped chapters, with new layouts and constraints along the way.

Your pace

No countdown or lives. Use undo and hints when you want a fresh approach.

3 stars

Finish without hints for the best rating. Replay boxes and keep your best result.

More than a perfect fit

Some orders ask for a stamped candy beneath a matching lid window. Later puzzles separate Berry and Mint: those aromas must not share an edge. Rotate, rearrange, and check the order before you seal the box.

A collection of small successes

Revisit completed gifts in your collection and follow your progress across Small Beginnings, Room to Breathe, Sweet Patterns, Open Spaces, and Final Flourish.

Made for a quiet break

Puzzle levels work offline. No player account is needed. Progress stays on your device, and music, sound and vibration have their own controls. Ads require internet access and may appear at completion milestones.
