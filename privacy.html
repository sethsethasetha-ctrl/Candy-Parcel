The game stores completed levels, best stars, saved puzzle arrangements, your gift collection, and music, sound, and haptics settings in local application storage. No player account is required. The game does not provide a cloud-sync service. Operating-system backups may include application data according to your device settings.

2. No advertising or tracking

The submitted build contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, tracking prompt, IDFA access, remote advertising configuration, third-party mediation service, or security service. 3. Device permissions

The game does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or precise location. You do not need to submit a name, email address, or payment information to play.

6. Children

Candy Parcel is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
