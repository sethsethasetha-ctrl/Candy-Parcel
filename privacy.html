Developer and operator: jiangfuqin. This policy describes Candy Parcel, a single-player candy-packing puzzle for iPhone and iPad.

Pre-release draft: the public support contact and final advertising/security partner disclosures must be completed before this policy is published or submitted to the App Store.

1. Progress stored on your device

The game stores completed levels, best stars, saved puzzle arrangements, your gift collection, and music, sound and vibration settings in its local application storage. No player account is required. The game does not provide its own cloud-sync service. Operating-system backups may include application data according to your device settings.

2. Advertising and service information

The app includes advertising and security services. These services may process device and network information, identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to deliver ads, operate services, measure their performance and prevent fraud. The advertising configuration service receives a request containing the app bundle identifier; as with internet requests, the service can receive network information such as an IP address.

The current integration uses Yandex Mobile Ads for completion-milestone interstitials and includes AppMetrica dependencies. Chartboost, Liftoff/Vungle and InMobi integrations are also included and may initialize when enabled by production configuration. The HSSecurity integration receives the app identifier and the device's identifier for vendor. Its final collection, retention and recipient details require confirmation from the service provider.

Partner privacy information: Yandex, Chartboost, Liftoff, and InMobi. Partner practices and the final App Store privacy disclosure must reflect the services used in the release build.

3. Tracking permission

On supported systems, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether you allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Our app reads the advertising identifier only when the system reports authorization, or under the applicable advertising-tracking setting on iOS 13. Your choice does not lock levels or change puzzle access. Security and advertising services may still initialize after a denied or restricted decision; initialization alone does not authorize tracking.

You can review your choice in iOS or iPadOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The system may not show a prompt if tracking is restricted or the decision has already been made. ATT permission is separate from any additional consent required for advertising data processing in your region.

4. Device permissions

The game does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or precise location. You do not need to submit a name, email address or payment information to play. If you contact support, the information you choose to send may be used to respond to your request.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you reset it in Settings or delete the app and its data. A device backup may retain a copy under your operating-system backup settings. Third-party service data may have different retention periods; consult the applicable provider policies. We cannot promise that resetting local progress also deletes data held by external services.

6. Privacy requests and regional rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy authority. A request can be assessed after the public support contact is available. For information held directly by a third-party provider, its own privacy request channel may also apply.

7. Children

Candy Parcel is not presented as a children's service or as an App Store Kids Category app. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should use the published support contact once available. Age-rating and advertising settings must be finalized before release.

8. Security and international processing

Device storage and service connections use the safeguards provided by their respective platforms and providers. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Advertising and service providers may process information in countries outside your residence, subject to their policies and applicable legal requirements.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or its services. The date above identifies this version.
